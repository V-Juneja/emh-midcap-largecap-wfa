# METHODOLOGY SECTION DRAFT

## 4. Methodology

### 4.1 Strategy Overview

This study employs a dual-universe mean reversion strategy designed to exploit pricing inefficiencies in Indian equity markets. The strategy is applied identically to both the NIFTY 100 large-cap universe and the NIFTY Midcap 150 mid-cap universe, allowing for direct comparison of performance across market efficiency segments.

### 4.2 Mean Reversion Strategy Design

#### 4.2.1 Core Signal Generation

The strategy utilizes a multi-factor mean reversion approach based on three primary indicators:

**1. Price Deviation from Moving Average**
- Calculate the 20-day simple moving average (SMA20) of closing prices
- Compute the percentage deviation: (Close - SMA20) / SMA20
- Generate buy signals when deviation falls below -2.5% (oversold condition)
- Generate sell signals when deviation exceeds +2.5% (overbought condition)

**2. RSI Mean Reversion**
- Calculate 14-day Relative Strength Index (RSI)
- Buy signal when RSI < 30 (oversold)
- Sell signal when RSI > 70 (overbought)

**3. Volume-Adjusted Price Extremes**
- Calculate 20-day average volume
- Identify extreme price movements on high volume days (>150% of average volume)
- Counter-trend positioning after extreme moves (fade strategy)

#### 4.2.2 Signal Combination Logic

Signals are combined using a weighted scoring system:
- Each indicator generates a score from -1 to +1
- Price deviation: weight 0.4
- RSI: weight 0.3  
- Volume extremes: weight 0.3
- Final signal = weighted sum of individual signals
- Trade execution threshold: |final signal| > 0.6

#### 4.2.3 Position Sizing and Risk Management

**Risk per Trade**: 2% of current portfolio equity
**Stop Loss**: 3x ATR (14-day Average True Range) from entry price
**Take Profit**: 1.5x risk (risk:reward ratio of 1:1.5)
**Maximum Portfolio Exposure**: 80% of capital
**Maximum Concurrent Positions**: 15 stocks per universe

#### 4.2.4 Entry and Exit Rules

**Entry Conditions**:
- All three indicators aligned (buy or sell)
- Sufficient liquidity: average daily volume > 1 million shares
- Price not in corporate action window (±5 days around ex-dividend dates)
- Stock not in existing position

**Exit Conditions**:
- Stop loss hit (3x ATR)
- Take profit hit (1.5x risk)
- Signal reversal (opposite signal generated)
- Maximum holding period of 20 trading days
- Portfolio rebalancing (monthly)

### 4.3 Backtesting Framework

#### 4.3.1 Transaction Cost Assumptions

**Brokerage**: 0.03% of trade value (both buy and sell)
**Securities Transaction Tax**: 0.025% on sell transactions
**Exchange Charges**: 0.003% of trade value
**GST**: 18% on brokerage and exchange charges
**Slippage**: 0.05% per trade (mid-cap), 0.02% per trade (large-cap)
**Total Impact**: ~0.15% per round trip (large-cap), ~0.18% per round trip (mid-cap)

#### 4.3.2 Portfolio Construction

**Equal Weighting**: Each position receives equal capital allocation
**Daily Rebalancing**: Portfolio value calculated at market close
**Compounding**: Returns reinvested daily
**Cash Management**: Uninvested capital earns risk-free rate (assumed 4% annually)

#### 4.3.3 Performance Measurement

**Primary Metrics**:
- Total Return (cumulative)
- Annualized Return
- Sharpe Ratio (risk-free rate = 4%)
- Sortino Ratio
- Maximum Drawdown
- Calmar Ratio

**Secondary Metrics**:
- Win Rate (percentage of profitable trades)
- Average Win/Loss Ratio
- Profit Factor
- Average Holding Period
- Portfolio Turnover
- Beta relative to respective index

### 4.4 Statistical Analysis Framework

#### 4.4.1 Hypothesis Testing

**Primary Hypothesis**: H0: Sharpe(mid-cap) ≤ Sharpe(large-cap)
**Alternative Hypothesis**: H1: Sharpe(mid-cap) > Sharpe(large-cap)

**Test Statistics**:
- Paired t-test on monthly returns between universes
- Bootstrap resampling for Sharpe ratio confidence intervals
- Kolmogorov-Smirnov test for return distribution differences
- Levene's test for variance equality

#### 4.4.2 Robustness Checks

**Time Period Analysis**:
- Sub-period analysis: 2015-2018, 2019-2022, 2023-2024
- Rolling window analysis (24-month rolling returns)
- Market regime analysis (bull vs bear markets)

**Parameter Sensitivity**:
- Moving average periods: 10, 15, 20, 25 days
- RSI periods: 7, 14, 21 days
- Deviation thresholds: ±1.5%, ±2.0%, ±2.5%, ±3.0%
- Stop loss multiples: 2x, 2.5x, 3x, 3.5x ATR

**Universe Composition Tests**:
- Top 50 vs bottom 50 stocks within each universe
- Sector-based sub-universes
- Liquidity-based filtering (high vs low volume stocks)

### 4.8 Expected Outcomes and Interpretation

#### 4.8.1 Success Criteria

**Statistical Significance**:
- Sharpe ratio difference significant at 5% level
- Consistent outperformance across sub-periods
- Robust to parameter variations
- Economic significance after costs

**Practical Significance**:
- Annual outperformance > 3% after costs
- Maximum drawdown < 25%
- Positive risk-adjusted returns in >70% of years
- Reasonable turnover (<200% annually)

#### 4.8.2 Result Interpretation Framework

**Efficiency Implications**:
- Superior mid-cap performance supports inefficiency hypothesis
- Similar performance suggests market integration
- Underperformance may indicate structural disadvantages

**Market Structure Insights**:
- Liquidity premium analysis
- Information dissemination speed
- Behavioral bias prevalence
- Institutional vs retail impact

This comprehensive methodology ensures rigorous testing of the mean reversion hypothesis while controlling for multiple sources of bias and providing robust statistical validation of results.
